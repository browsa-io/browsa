# Code of conduct

This repository is a place to work on the Browsa SDKs. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests and reviews, is expected to:

- Be respectful. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Be specific. Say what you saw, what you expected and how to reproduce it.
- Assume good faith, and accept correction when you are wrong.

Not accepted: harassment, insults, discrimination of any kind, sexual language or imagery, publishing someone's private information, and spam.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and block accounts that break these rules.

To report a problem, write to <support@browsa.io>. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept private.
